VULNET FINTECH KNOW-YOUR-CUSTOMER (KYC) VERIFICATION POLICY

1. Customer Identification Program (CIP):
Every account holder must undergo verified identity validation prior to initiating external fund transfers.
Verification standards adhere to standard FinTech compliance frameworks.

2. Primary Identification Documents:
- Unexpired Government Photo ID: Passport, Driver's License, National Identity Card, or State ID.
- Taxpayer Identification Number: SSN, ITIN, PAN, or applicable national tax identifier.

3. Proof of Address Standards:
- Utility bill, lease agreement, or credit institution statement issued within the preceding 90 days.
- Documents showing P.O. boxes or commercial maildrops are rejected.

4. Periodic KYC Re-verification:
- Retail customers are re-verified every 12 months.
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) applies to high-volume accounts and PEP (Politically Exposed Persons).
