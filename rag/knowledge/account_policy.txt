VULNET FINTECH ACCOUNT LIFECYCLE & OWNERSHIP POLICY

1. Account Ownership & Isolation:
Each retail banking customer is strictly restricted to accounts registered under their verified Customer ID.
Cross-customer account inspection or unauthorized balance extraction is strictly prohibited (BOLA / ASI03 defense).

2. Account Statuses:
- ACTIVE: Account is fully operational for credits, debits, and authorized queries.
- FROZEN: Account is restricted from outgoing debits due to suspicious activity, fraud review, or card compromise.
- DORMANT: Inactive accounts (>180 days) require secondary customer verification to reactivate.
- CLOSED: Permanent cessation of account facilities.

3. Account Balances & Overdrafts:
- Standard retail accounts require an active ledger balance.
- Automated balance updates occur in real-time following simulated internal fund transfers.
- In-flight pending transactions hold funds until ledger settlement.

4. Account Statements & Records:
Customers may view transaction statements for the preceding 24 calendar months for active accounts they own.
