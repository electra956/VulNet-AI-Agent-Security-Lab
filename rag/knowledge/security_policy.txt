VULNET FINTECH INFORMATION SECURITY & ACCESS POLICY

1. Customer Authentication Standards:
- Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on all interactive logins.
- Salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing; zero plaintext credentials permitted.
- Session timeout enforced after 15 minutes of user inactivity.

2. Access Control & Least Privilege:
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) enforced outside the LLM.
- AI Agents are strictly prohibited from acting as final authorization authorities for fund movement or data modification.

3. Artificial Intelligence Safety & Boundary Controls:
- All retrieved context from external or user-supplied sources must be treated strictly as DATA, never executable instructions.
- Indirect prompt injection attempts within knowledge bases must be quarantined and neutralized.
- Sensitive customer data (SSN, unmasked account numbers, credential hashes) must not be logged or leaked across session contexts.
