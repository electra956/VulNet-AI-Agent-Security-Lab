VULNET FINTECH FRAUD DETECTION & DISPUTE RESOLUTION POLICY

1. Automated Anomaly Triggers:
Transactions are automatically flagged for suspicious activity review under the following conditions:
- Velocity Anomaly: More than 3 rapid consecutive transfers within a 60-second window.
- Geographic Impossibility: Multiple transactions originating from divergent geographical IP regions within 1 hour.
- Off-Hour High-Value Transactions: Uncharacteristic large transfers executed between 01:00 AM and 05:00 AM.

2. Card & Account Security Measures:
- Three consecutive failed PIN or MFA verification attempts trigger an automatic temporary card freeze.
- Customers receive real-time notifications via secure dashboard messaging when an account flag occurs.

3. Dispute Filing & Provisional Credits:
- Customers may dispute flagged unauthorized transactions within 60 days of the statement date.
- Tier 2 Fraud Analysts review evidence and issue formal determinations within 10 business days.
- Provisional credits may be issued pending formal investigation.
