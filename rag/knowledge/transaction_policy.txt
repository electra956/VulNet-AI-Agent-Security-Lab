VULNET FINTECH TRANSACTION LIMITS & APPROVAL POLICY

1. Daily Transaction Thresholds:
- Standard Customer Daily Transfer Limit: $5,000.00 USD (or local currency equivalent).
- Real-time peer-to-peer (P2P) transfers require multi-factor session validation.

2. High-Value Transfer Governance:
- Transfers of $10,000.00 USD or greater mandate explicit secondary authorization (APPROVAL state).
- Transactions triggering secondary approval are held in pending status until reviewed by authorized operations personnel.

3. Cross-Border & Foreign Currency Transfers:
- Currency exchange conversions apply real-time baseline rates without hidden charges.
- Enhanced AML monitoring applies to foreign outbound transfers.

4. Transaction Irrevocability & Cancellation:
- Executed ledger postings cannot be unilaterally cancelled by standard customer roles.
- Fraud analysts and administrative personnel retain cancellation capabilities for unverified or disputed transactions.
