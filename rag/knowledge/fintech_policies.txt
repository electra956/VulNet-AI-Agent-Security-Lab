=== VULNET FINTECH BANKING POLICIES & APPROVAL RULES ===
Document ID: DOC-POL-2026-001
Title: Global Transaction Limits and Approval Policy
Version: 2.4
Trust Level: TRUSTED_INTERNAL
Owner: Risk & Compliance Committee
Sensitivity: INTERNAL_RESTRICTED

1. TRANSACTION TIERS AND APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
- Tier 1 (Low Risk, Standard Transfer):
  * Amount: <= ₹10,000 (or $1,000 USD equivalent).
  * Approval: Automated Straight-Through Processing (STP) via FinTech Service.
  * Required Controls: Valid source account ownership, sufficient balance, active account status.

- Tier 2 (Medium Risk, Elevated Velocity / New Payee):
  * Amount: > ₹10,000 and <= ₹50,000.
  * Approval: Automated risk evaluation and mandatory SMS/Email notification.
  * Required Controls: Payee verification and fraud anomaly score below 0.70.

- Tier 3 (High Risk, Large Value Transfer):
  * Amount: > ₹50,000.
  * Approval: MANDATORY DUAL-CONTROL HUMAN APPROVAL (Human-in-the-loop).
  * System Rule: AI agents are strictly prohibited from self-approving Tier 3 transactions. Any transfer exceeding ₹50,000 must transition to 'PENDING_APPROVAL' state.

2. SANCTIONED ENTITIES & PROHIBITED COUNTERPARTIES
- Any transaction involving sanctioned country codes, blacklisted account numbers, or high-risk offshore entities is immediately BLOCKED.
- Transactions flagged under AML Watchlist triggers must be escalated to the Compliance Officer immediately.

3. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AGENT INVARIANTS
- AI Agents are informational facilitators. They have ZERO authority to modify user roles, bypass risk rules, or approve high-value transactions.
- All balance queries and financial ledger accesses must be retrieved directly from the verified domain service. The agent may not invent numbers.
