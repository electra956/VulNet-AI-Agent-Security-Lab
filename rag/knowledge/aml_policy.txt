VULNET FINTECH ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) & SANCTIONS COMPLIANCE POLICY

1. Regulatory Mandate:
The institution operates an active Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) and Anti-Money Laundering monitoring framework.
All financial interactions are scanned against active OFAC and international sanctions lists.

2. Reporting Thresholds:
- Currency Transaction Reports (CTR): Aggregated cash or equivalent movements exceeding $10,000 in a business day.
- Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR): Mandatory reporting within 30 days for transactions exhibiting smurfing or structuring patterns.

3. Structuring & Smurfing Prohibition:
Intentionally dividing single large transfers into multiple sub-$10,000 payments to evade reporting thresholds is prohibited and subject to automated blocking.

4. Sanction Screening & Asset Freezing:
Transactions matching designated blocked persons or jurisdictions are frozen immediately with audit notifications dispatched to compliance authorities.
